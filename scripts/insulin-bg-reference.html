Workbook tip · 2.5% dextrose

Dextrose support—including 2.5% when sufficient—can help support continued prescribed insulin for ketone clearance. Your workbook lists 2.5% for BG 250–299 and 150–249 mg/dL. Use the prescribed concentration and mL/hr together to provide the required glucose; 2.5% is an option, not an automatic fixed-rate dose rule.

Symptomatic or severe hypoglycemia requires immediate treatment and insulin reassessment under the hypoglycemia protocol. Tube placement or enteral support must not delay correction. NG support is adjunctive nutrition, not a validated equivalent substitute for IV glucose rescue.
