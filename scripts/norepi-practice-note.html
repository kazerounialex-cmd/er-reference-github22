Best practice · syringe-pump setup

Use a compatible syringe pump for a diluted, titratable infusion; verify syringe brand/size, line setup and pump performance at the chosen rate. Keep the prepared concentration fixed when titrating an existing syringe.

Our preparation defaults: concentration-based mode, 0.05 mcg/kg/min, 15 mcg/mL and 12 mL final. These are local workflow preferences, not universal veterinary standards. A canine clinical pilot study used patient-specific dilution so 0.1 mcg/kg/min initially ran at 1 mL/hr; it did not establish an optimal syringe size or flow rate. Target-pump-rate preparation remains available as an alternative; in concentration-based mode, pump rate and run time are calculated from the patient’s weight and selected dose. Lyons, Hess & Silverstein, 2025.

Connect the extension set close to the catheter to limit delivery delay; account for priming and residual volume. Prime/purge before connecting to the patient.
